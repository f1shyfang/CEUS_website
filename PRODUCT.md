# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

CEUS members and prospective UNSW chemical engineering students use the public site to find credible, practical updates and guidance. Society executives use the existing admin dashboard to keep content current without developer support. Visitors should be able to quickly discover an update, a student guide, or a careers-and-industry article, then read it without distraction.

## Product Purpose

The CEUS website is the public home of the Chemical Engineering Undergraduate Society at UNSW. Its blog extends that role into a durable, searchable library of society news, student guidance, and industry information. Success is a calm, trustworthy reading experience and a publishing workflow that lets executives maintain it independently.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, knowledgeable, purposeful. CEUS should feel like a well-kept study journal: approachable for new students, credible for industry-facing material, and energetic enough to reflect an active student society.

## Anti-references

Avoid a glossy news portal, an overcrowded magazine grid, social-media mechanics, paywalls, comments, and newsletter-first conversion patterns. Avoid generic editorial affectation, grey-heavy cards, ornamental rounded panels, and copied Substack branding.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next useful read obvious.
2. Let the writing carry the experience.
3. Connect current society life with durable student knowledge.
4. Keep publishing approachable for society executives.
5. Make every reader, including keyboard and assistive-technology users, feel considered.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction expectations. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic content structure, descriptive image alternatives, responsive reflow, and reduced-motion preferences. Use Australian English in reader-facing CEUS copy.
